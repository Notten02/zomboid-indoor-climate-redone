# Credits

## Original author

**Frozen_Heart** made Indoor Climate (EHR Addon), shown in game as Indoor Climate Lite
([Workshop 3772549209](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3772549209), mod ID
`IndoorClimateLiteB42`). Indoor Climate Redone is a rewrite of that mod and follows its design: the room simulation
and its numbers, the four devices, the sandbox options, the public API, and the English and Russian texts.

Frozen_Heart opened the mod to the community and asked only to be credited as the original author:

> Feel free to modify, fix, edit, whatever. I don't own these mods, the community does. The only thing I ask is that
> you credit me as the original author

## This rewrite

The code of Indoor Climate Redone is new, by Nick Otten and contributors, under the MIT licence (`LICENSE`).

## Reused content

The models, textures, icons, item scripts, sandbox option texts and translations come from the original mod. They
are credited to it and are not relicensed under MIT. `ASSETS.md` lists every reused file and what was changed on
import. The original's Workshop page and README are archived in `docs/original-author-guides/`.
